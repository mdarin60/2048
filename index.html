Join the numbers and get to the 2048 tile!
Arrows move tiles across a layer, Q/E (or Page Up/Page Down) move them up/down. Drag to spin the cube, scroll to zoom inside. When it fills up, use X-ray, F to explode the layers, or 1–4 to focus one layer (0 for all). G hides the direction labels.

How to play: Use your arrow keys to move the tiles across the layers and Q / E to move them up and down the cube. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one!

Note: This site is the official version of 2048. You can play it on your phone via http://git.io/2048. All other apps or sites are derivatives or fakes, and should be used with caution.
